Pretty much all my repo's are mirrored to GitHub, GitLab and Codeberg, and some repo's are mirrored to the {du}punkto git server.

I like other things besides coding, such as graphic design, music, playing a nice game, occasionally writing (sometimes even for Wikipedia!), some photography, and reading (trying to read more, currently reading Fahrenheit 451 by Ray Bradbury).
