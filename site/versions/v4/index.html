Haii!

Welcome! I'm Gijs (/ɣɛis/), also known as gijs6, gijsz, ggijs, and many other variations. I'm a 17-year-old developer, part-time overthinker, professional procrastinator, and full-time enthusiastic mess from Schiedam (in the Netherlands), and this is my little corner of the interwebs.

About me

I don't have a clue how to describe myself, so please annoy other people by asking them about me instead. They'll probably have a much more accurate answer anyway. But since you somehow ended up on my website, you probably already know me (or maybe I really am famous).

Here's what I'm up to right now:

  • studying 5vwo (which I think is roughly comparable to year 12 in the UK and 11th grade in the US) at Lyceum Schravenlant, doing natuur & techniek, which means physics, chemistry, biology (unfortunately), NLT, and français (bien sûr!)
  • working as a junior developer at Qdentity
  • being a member of the medezeggenschapsraad (school council) at my school
  • procrastinating on choosing my next study
  • messing around with just about anything that seems remotely interesting

I'm also part of {du}punkto, a small collective that I run with three friends. We mostly make things, break things, talk about making things, and occasionally actually finish something. It's a lot of fun!

I mainly use (the best language of all) Python, on Arch Linux btw, but I also occasionally use Elixir and, sadly, JavaScript.

Pretty much all the code I write is open-source and publicly available for you to judge see. Some of my projects are available on the projects page, although I have a tendency to start more projects than I finish. Pretty much all my repos are mirrored to GitHub, GitLab, and Codeberg, and some repos are also mirrored to the {du}punkto git server.

I also enjoy graphic design, listening to music, playing a nice game every once in a while, and generally messing around with computers.

I occasionally write too (usually when I have an unnecessarily complicated opinion about something). Sometimes I even write for Wikipedia!
